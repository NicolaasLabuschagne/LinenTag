Get your tags

NFC tag kits are coming soon. In the meantime, print labels for your bedding. Upgrading later takes a few minutes.

Upgrading from printed labels

  1. Sew one tag into a seam or hem of each piece.
  2. In the LinenTag app, open Settings → NFC tags → Upgrade my bedding.
  3. Scan a piece's printed label, then tap its new tag with your phone. Move on to the next piece; no menus in between.

New to LinenTag? Sew in the tags, then in the app tap Add new bedding, or open any item and tap Link a tag.
