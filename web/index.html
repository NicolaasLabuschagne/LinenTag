How it works

  1. Scan the label on each piece of new bedding. LinenTag fills in what it is and groups each set together.
  2. Tap Use when a set goes on the bed. The one coming off goes to the laundry.
  3. After washing, tap Put away laundry and scan the pieces. LinenTag shows what belongs together and where it goes.
