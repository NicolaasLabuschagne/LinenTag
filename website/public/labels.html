Labels for bedding you already own

Bought your sheets before LinenTag? Print a label for each piece, attach it, then scan each label in the app with Add new bedding. One scan per piece and you're done.

4. Print and attach

  • Print on fabric labels made for washing (iron-on or sew-on printable fabric sheets). Paper labels won't survive the wash.
  • Put each label on an inside corner or hem, where it won't rub against you. Iron it flat, or stitch around the edge.
  • Try one label through a wash first. If it gets hard to scan, print a fresh one: in the app, open the item and tap Link a tag.
  • Want something permanent? Sew-in NFC tags last through every wash, and upgrading from these labels takes minutes.
